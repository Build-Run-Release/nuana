Privacy notice

Nuana does not sell mood or wardrobe data. Core preferences remain in your browser until you explicitly use server features.

Data used

Presentation, manual weather, occasion, wardrobe, and recently worn preferences are saved in this browser only if you turn on “Remember my preferences on this device.” Saved looks, history, and feedback are written to the local server only after you use those features.

Location and weather

Using browser location requires your permission. If you decline or location is unavailable, you can optionally search for a city. Nuana sends consented coordinates or the city you entered to Open-Meteo to retrieve current weather and a short forecast. Nuana does not store those coordinates or city searches, and does not send them to advertisers. You can also use manual weather settings without sharing a location.

Local context

Your browser's language and time zone are used to determine local time of day. A season is shown only when Nuana has a hemisphere from a location lookup. These context values are used to rank recommendations and are not saved to your account.

Your controls

You may export or delete local saved looks, history, feedback, wardrobe preferences, and account data from the Privacy controls.

Health and safety

Mood selections are styling inputs, not medical, psychological, or diagnostic information. Clothing recommendations are for inspiration only.
